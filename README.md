# Aging Population in Thailand — Dataset for Research, Education, and Practice

This repository contains anonymized demographic data used in the study:  

**Grassi, S. (2024) Analyzing Aging Population in Thailand. [ResearchGate Link](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/386051069_Analyzing_Aging_Population_in_Thailand)**

The dataset is intended for **students, academics, researchers, and practitioners** interested in exploring demographic trends and analyzing an aging society using **descriptive analytics, KPI mapping, and business intelligence insights**.  

---

## **Dataset Description**

- **`df_aging_pop.csv`**: Demographic data of Thailand’s population from 2002 to 2021, segmented by:  
  - Age groups (e.g., 60–69, 70–79, 80+)  
  - Gender  
  - Geographic region (central, north, northeast/Issan, south)  

- **Research Questions You Can Explore**:
  1. What are the trends in the elderly population growth segmented by age group in Thailand?  
  2. What are the trends in the elderly population segmented by geographic area?  
  3. How is the elderly population distributed regionally across Thailand?  
  4. What is the gender distribution among the elderly population?  

- **Geospatial Files (`THA_AdminBoundaries_candidate.gdb`)**: Optional files for advanced users who want to visualize regional distributions with GeoPandas or GIS tools.

---

## **Data Sources**

1. **Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX)** — Geospatial data at the regional level, licensed under [CC BY-IGO](https://data.humdata.org/).  
2. **National Statistics Office of Thailand (NSO, 2022)** — Demographic data accessed via the [Government Data Catalog](https://data.go.th/en/blog) Smart Plus API.  

These datasets provide a trustworthy overview of Thailand’s aging population, detailing composition, geographic distribution, and growth trends. All data are aggregated and anonymized; no sensitive information is included.
